ABOUT US

At Cosmicookiess, we believe that the smallest accessories can carry the biggest emotions. 💖

We are a Chennai-based handmade brand creating customised bracelets, charms, and gifting pieces designed to make every moment special. Each product is carefully handcrafted with attention to detail, ensuring that every piece is unique, meaningful, and made just for you.

Whether it's a gift for a loved one, a friendship token, or something special for yourself — we turn your ideas into something beautiful you can wear and cherish. Our goal is simple: to offer affordable, aesthetic, and personalised accessories that connect people and create memories.
